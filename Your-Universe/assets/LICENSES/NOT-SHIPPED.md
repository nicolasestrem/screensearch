# What this build ships and what it does not

`SOURCES.md` is generated by `tools/fetch-assets.mjs`; this file is not, so it survives a re-fetch.

## Shipped

`assets/manifest.json` and the six `assets/*.glb` packs are built by `tools/pack-assets.mjs` from
these Kenney kits only:

- kenney-city-kit-commercial
- kenney-city-kit-suburban
- kenney-city-kit-roads
- kenney-car-kit
- kenney-nature-kit
- kenney-mini-characters

Every model in them is CC0 1.0 and the licence text for each is the matching `kenney-*.txt` in this
folder.

## Staged but not shipped: the Quaternius packs

The three `quaternius-*` packs are downloaded, and their licence evidence is kept here, but **no
Quaternius model is used by the page**. quaternius.com now serves the Quaternius Asset License v1.0
(not CC0) and its pack archives, on Google Drive, contain no glTF files at all, while poly.pizza
still labels the same models CC0 1.0. That contradiction is the owner's to resolve, so cycle 19
shipped Kenney only. Deleting these three files would not change what the page draws.
`quaternius-ultimate-buildings.txt`, `quaternius-cars.txt` and `quaternius-nature.txt` record the
exact pages and the licence text as served.
